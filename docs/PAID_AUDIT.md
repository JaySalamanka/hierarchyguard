## Intake boundary

By default the Action must not publish files, filenames, repository identity,
row values, or document contents. The workflow owner may explicitly opt into
GitHub-hosted finding details; the risks must remain documented. A future CTA
may send only generic counts, score, rule codes, and tool version to an approved
HTTPS intake page. Customer files are accepted only after terms,
confidentiality, data handling, price, and scope are confirmed through a private
channel. Do not send files, credentials, or customer information through issues,
pull requests, discussions, security advisories, or an unapproved email address.
If intake opens, an approved HTTPS page must present the final scope, terms,
privacy and retention notices, payment process, and secure upload instructions.
