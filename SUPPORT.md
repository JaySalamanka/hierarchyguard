# Support

This private launch candidate has no public support channel, commitment, or
service-level agreement.

Public issues, discussions, and audit intake remain disabled until the owner
approves a support channel, privacy notice, commercial terms, and data-handling
workflow. Never attach customer hierarchy files to a public issue.

If a public release is approved without a support channel, the free Action will
be documentation-only and provided without a support SLA. Human hierarchy
review, correction planning, vendor compatibility, source-evidence work, and
delivery assistance remain unavailable until a separate paid-service launch is
approved.
