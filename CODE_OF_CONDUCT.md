# Community conduct

AssetTree CI discussions must remain professional, safe, and useful.

Participants are expected to address ideas and evidence rather than attack
people; respect different backgrounds and levels of experience; avoid
harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and disclosure
of confidential customer data; and follow repository security and data-handling
instructions.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove content and participation that
breaks these expectations. Serious safety or legal concerns may be escalated to
the relevant platform or authority. Enforcement decisions should be documented
privately and applied consistently.

Until a public conduct contact and community channel are approved, external
community participation is not open and this private launch candidate accepts
no public conduct reports.
